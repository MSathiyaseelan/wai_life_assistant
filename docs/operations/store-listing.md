# Store listing (ASO)

Copy for the Google Play listing of `com.wai.lifeassistant`, keyword-tuned for
store search. Paste into **Play Console → (select app) → Grow users → Store presence →
Store listings → Default store listing** (or search "store listing" in the
console's search bar). English only. Listing changes don't need a new build.

Play limits: title 30 chars, short description 80, full description 4000.
Play policy bans "best", "#1", "free", prices, and emoji/ALL CAPS in the title.

Target keywords, roughly in priority order (long-tail first; big generic terms
like "expense tracker" won't rank until the app has ratings):
AI assistant, family organizer, household organizer, family expense tracker, grocery list,
meal planner, pantry, reminders, bill splitter, birthday reminder,
function / event planner, wedding gift tracker, moi / gift register,
vehicle service reminder.

RiyasHome: AI Family Organizer

Family expense tracker, grocery list, meal plan & reminders with an AI assistant

**Full description**

```
RiyasHome is an AI-powered family organizer that keeps your household's money, groceries, tasks, functions and plans in one app — just talk to it.

💬 JUST ASK
Type or speak naturally — "spent 500 on groceries" or "remind me to call the doctor tomorrow" — and the AI assistant turns it into the right entry in the right place. Voice input works in English, Tamil, Hindi and 7 more Indian languages.

🎊 FUNCTIONS & EVENT TRACKER
Weddings, house-warmings, naming ceremonies, birthdays — plan your own functions and keep track of the ones you attend.
• Your own functions: plan everything in one place — guest list, catering menu, clothing gifts for families, return gifts, essentials like attire, decor and pooja items, and vendors with advance paid and balance due.
• Gift & cash register (moi): record who gave what at your function — cash, gold/silver and gift items — with name, place and relation. Add entries one by one or in bulk, or import from CSV.
• Know what you owe: mark gifts as returned when you attend their function, and see what's still pending and the full history with every family.
• Functions you attend: save upcoming invitations with date and venue, plan the gift with your family, and keep a record of what you gave.

💰 EXPENSE TRACKER & BUDGET
Track income, expenses and monthly budgets for yourself or the whole family. Scan a bill and let AI fill in the amount and category. See where the money goes with simple reports.

🤝 BILL SPLITTER
Split shared costs with your household, flatmates or a trip group. Keep track of who owes what, lend and borrow, and settle up without awkward reminders.

🛒 GROCERY LIST, PANTRY & MEAL PLANNER
Keep a shared grocery list the whole family can add to. Know what's already in the kitchen before you shop, plan the week's meals, and save your recipes — RiyasHome checks which ingredients you have in stock.

✅ TASKS & REMINDERS
Quick-add to-dos and reminders, assign them to family members with @, and get notified on time. Repeat reminders daily, weekly or monthly.

📅 BIRTHDAYS, NOTES & BILLS
Never miss a birthday or anniversary. Jot quick notes, and track recurring bills before they're due.

👕 WARDROBE, GARAGE & MORE
Track your wardrobe, vehicle service and insurance dates, and where you put things — the small stuff that adds up.

👨‍👩‍👧 BUILT FOR FAMILIES
Create a family group and share wallets, grocery lists, tasks and plans with your partner, parents or kids. Everyone sees updates in real time. Keep personal things private and share only what you choose.

🔒 PRIVATE & SECURE
Lock the app with your fingerprint or a PIN. Your data is yours — export it or delete your account at any time.

Download RiyasHome and bring your family's everyday life into one place.
```
