🗑️ Account Deletion

Delete Your RiyasHome Account

Applies to RiyasHome app (Android & iOS) · See also our Privacy Policy
A

Delete from within the app

The fastest way to delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Open the RiyasHome app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
Your account is deactivated immediately and all personal records are soft-deleted the moment you confirm — you do not need to wait for a response from us.
B

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or can't sign in, email us from the address linked to your account and we'll process the deletion on your behalf:

✉️ riyasailabs@gmail.com

We verify the request against your registered phone number and complete it within 7 business days.
